Frequently Asked Questions

To cancel or modify your booking, go to your Booking History page or contact our support team with your booking reference. Changes are subject to availability and fare conditions.
AeroNepal checkout currently uses eSewa's official UAT test environment for demonstration. No card details or real-money payments are accepted in the booking flow.
Each passenger is allowed 15 kg of checked baggage and 5 kg of cabin baggage on domestic flights. Excess baggage fees apply beyond these limits.
Refunds depend on the fare type purchased. Non-refundable tickets may receive partial credit for future bookings. Contact our support team for specific refund queries.
In case of delays, we'll notify you via your registered email. If a flight is cancelled, you'll be offered an alternative flight or a full refund.
We recommend arriving at least 1.5 hours before your domestic flight departure. Counter check-in closes 30 minutes before departure.
